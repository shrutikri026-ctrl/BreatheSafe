📋 3 Simple steps for your day
🌅 Morning (Before 10 AM)
Seal doors & commute with N95
Cold morning air traps vehicle smoke at street level. Keep doors and windows closed. If commuting by scooter or walking, wear a tightly fitted N95 mask.
☀️ Afternoon (1:30 - 3:00 PM)
Safe air window: air out rooms
Afternoon sunshine warms the ground and temporarily lifts the smog blanket. Crack open windows for 15-20 minutes to let fresh oxygen in. Do any outdoor chores now.
🌙 Evening & Night
Bedtime clean air routine
Smog settles back down after sunset. Wet-mop bedroom floors to catch dust. Keep an air purifier or DIY filter running in the bedroom, and place a small bowl of water near the heater so your throat doesn't dry out.

🛠️ The 15-Minute DIY Air Purifier (Corsi-Rosenthal Box)

Commercial air purifiers cost $300+. You can make one yourself for under $40 that cleans your room 3x faster!

What You Need ($35 - $45)
Simple Materials
• 1 standard 20-inch box fan ($20)
• 4 furnace filters (MERV 13 grade, $20)
• 1 roll of duct tape + cardboard box
How to Build (15 Minutes)
Tape Filters in a Cube
1. Stand the 4 filters in a square cube with airflow arrows pointing inward.
2. Tape a cardboard bottom.
3. Place the box fan on top, blowing clean air upward into the room.
4. Tape all edges tight.
Why It Works
Cleaner Than $300 Units
Having 4 large filters lets massive amounts of air pass through without motor strain. It scrubs a bedroom in just 15 minutes!
💡 4 Free Home Hacks (Cost $0)
Cleaning Hack
1. Always Wet-Mop (Never Dry Sweep)
Sweeping with a dry broom kicks fine smoke dust right back up into your breathing zone. Wipe floors with a damp cloth or wet mop to permanently trap dust.
💡 Tip: Add a drop of mild dish soap to the mop water to trap dust faster.
Draft Sealing Hack
2. Rolled Towel Door Snake
Winter smoke seeps in through the gap under entrance and balcony doors. Roll an old bathroom towel and press it firmly against the door bottom to stop smoke leaks.
💡 Tip: Tape plastic bubble wrap to drafty windows to insulate against cold and smoke.
Kitchen Hack
3. Cook on Back Burners
Frying or gas cooking can spike indoor smoke to 500+ AQI. Always cook on the rear burners under your exhaust fan, and keep pots covered with lids.
💡 Tip: Turn on the kitchen exhaust 2 minutes before lighting the stove.
Breathing Hack
4. Simple Water Bowl on Heater
Room heaters make air bone-dry, paralyzing your nasal filters. Place a simple metal or ceramic bowl of water on top of your radiator to gently add moisture.
💡 Warning: Don't use tap water in ultrasonic mist makers; it creates white mineral dust!
🌱 2 Important Clean Swaps
Heating Safety
🚫 Never sleep with coal/wood fires indoors
Burning coal (*angithi*) or wood in a closed bedroom creates invisible, deadly Carbon Monoxide (CO). Use an electric oil heater or hot water bottles to stay warm safely.
Neighborhood Action
🍂 Compost dry leaves instead of burning
Burning a pile of dried leaves fills the street with thick cancer-causing smoke. Rake leaves around tree bases as natural mulch instead.
